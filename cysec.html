Legal & Ethical Foundation — Read Before Phase 3
All offensive techniques in this roadmap require explicit written authorisation from the system owner. Practise only in your personal lab, on designated platforms (TryHackMe, HackTheBox, VulnHub), or in authorised engagements. Unauthorised access is a criminal offence under the IT Act 2000 (India), the Computer Fraud and Abuse Act (USA), the Computer Misuse Act (UK), and equivalent laws worldwide. Phase 3 covers this in full before any attack tool appears.
