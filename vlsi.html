Master Software List (All Free or Student)
Broke & Ambitious Strategy: Use your student email for Autodesk Fusion 360 (free), GitHub Student Pack (tons of free tools), Onshape Education (free), and JetBrains IDEs (free). For actual chip fabrication, check out Google's OpenMPW shuttle — they literally fab your chip for free. For PCBs, JLCPCB makes 10 PCBs for ~$2. RTL-SDR dongles cost $25 and unlock the entire RF world.
Built for the ambitious and broke. Every tool listed is free or free with a student email.
